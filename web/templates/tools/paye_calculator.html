2026 Kenya PAYE, SHIF, NSSF & Housing Levy Guide

In Kenya's 2026 statutory payroll framework, KRA taxes monthly chargeable income across progressive bands (10% on the first KES 24,000; 25% on the next KES 8,333; 30% up to KES 500,000; 32.5% up to KES 800,000; and 35% above KES 800,000) minus Personal Relief of KES 2,400/month. Mandatory NSSF (6% up to Tier II cap), SHIF (2.75% of gross), and Affordable Housing Levy (1.5% of gross) reduce taxable income before PAYE is applied.
