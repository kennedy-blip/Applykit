Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Last updated: November 2026

🔒 Core Guarantee: We DO NOT train AI models on your CV, cover letters, or private notes. Raw CV text submitted for instant checks is processed ephemerally and only SHA-256 hashes are logged for abuse/quota prevention.

1. Data We Collect & Minimize

When you sign in via Google OAuth (Supabase Auth), we store your email, display name, subscription tier, and any job applications you choose to save to your Kanban tracker. All tables enforce strict PostgreSQL Row Level Security (auth.uid() = user_id).

2. Google AdSense & Cookie Disclosure

ApplyKit's Free tier is supported by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or selecting "Essential Only" in our consent banner. ApplyKit Pro subscribers see zero third-party advertising scripts.

3. Payments Security (M-Pesa & Lemon Squeezy)

M-Pesa PINs are entered exclusively on your Safaricom SIM Toolkit and never touch ApplyKit servers. Card payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, Paystack, or Flutterwave as Merchant of Record.
